Using the stopwatch in lessons

Press Start to begin counting and Lap to record a split without stopping – perfect for timing laps of the field, shuttle runs or each stage of an experiment. The fastest lap shows in green and the slowest in red once you have three or more.

Questions

How accurate is the stopwatch?

It shows hundredths of a second and is based on your device’s clock, so it stays accurate even if you switch tabs.

Can I record lap times?

Yes. Press Lap (or the L key) as often as you like. Each lap shows its own time and the running total.
