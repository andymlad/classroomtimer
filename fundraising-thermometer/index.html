Using the fundraising thermometer

  1. Type the name of your appeal and your target.
  2. Add donations as they come in – use the quick buttons, type an amount, or count the cash tin with the cash counter.
  3. Go full screen in assembly and watch the thermometer rise. When you reach the target there’s confetti and a cheer!

Not raising money? Choose No currency to count anything else – books read, laps run, kind acts or house points.

Questions

Is my fundraising total saved?

Yes – it’s saved in your own browser on this device, so it’s still there next time you open the page on the same computer.

Can I count coins and notes?

Yes. The cash counter lists every UK coin and note (and dollars or euros if you switch currency). Type how many of each you have and add the total to the thermometer in one tap.
