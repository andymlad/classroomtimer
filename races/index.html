Emoji race & duck race

Type your names, pick an emoji race or a rubber duck race, and press Start. The winner is chosen fairly at random – the race just makes picking exciting.

Keyboard: Space start · F full screen.

How the races work

When you press Start, the finishing order is decided fairly at random by your device’s secure random number generator. The racers then speed up and slow down along the way, so the lead changes and nobody knows who will win until the end. Tick Remove the winner after each race to pick a different person each time – handy for choosing who goes next.

Ideas

  • Pick who answers the next question or who goes first.
  • Choose the winning house or team for a prize.
  • A fun duck race for a school fair or fundraiser.

Questions

Is the winner really random?

Yes. The finishing order is chosen at the start using your browser’s secure random number generator, so every racer has exactly the same chance.

How many racers can there be?

Up to 12. Type one name per line, press “Use icon names” to name them after the racers (Apple, Tractor, Red monster…), or press “Use numbers” to number them.
