Fun timers for kids

Visual countdowns children understand without reading the numbers – race an animal to the finish line, watch the sand run out, or wait for the traffic light to turn red.

Which fun timer should I use?

🐢 Animal race – great for ages 3 to 6

Children pick a tortoise, rabbit, snail, duck, dinosaur or ladybird and watch it walk to the finish flag as time runs down. Flowers along the track burst into bloom as the animal passes them, so even children who can’t read numbers can see how far there is to go. At the finish there’s confetti and a cheer.

⏳ Sand timer

A classic hourglass – the sand drains into a growing heap. Calm and familiar for quiet work and reading.

🚦 Traffic light

Green while there’s plenty of time, amber at half time and red near the end. A simple signal that works well for transitions and tidy-up time.

🕯️ Candle

The candle burns down and goes out with a wisp of smoke when time is up – lovely for story time and quiet reflection.

Questions

What age are the fun timers for?

They suit nursery, Reception and Key Stage 1 especially, but older children enjoy them too. The animal race is designed for 3 to 6 year olds.

Can I link straight to one fun timer?

Yes. Add #race, #sand, #traffic or #candle to the address, for example classroomtimer.co.uk/fun-timers/#sand.

Can I change the time?

Yes. Choose a preset or tap the numbers to type any time from a few seconds up to 6 hours.
