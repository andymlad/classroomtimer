Countdowns included

Pick a country to see its own holidays: the United Kingdom, Ireland, the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, India, France, Germany, Spain and Italy.

For the UK that’s Christmas, New Year, Pancake Day, Mothering Sunday, Easter, the bank holidays, Father’s Day, Halloween, Bonfire Night and Remembrance Sunday. Other countries get their own days, such as Thanksgiving and Independence Day in the USA, St Patrick’s Day in Ireland, ANZAC Day in Australia and New Zealand, and Bastille Day in France. Each one always counts to the next time that date comes round.

How many sleeps?

For younger children the countdown also shows the number of sleeps – how many nights until the big day – which is much easier to understand than hours and minutes.

Questions

How many sleeps until Christmas?

Open the Christmas Day countdown – it shows the number of sleeps (nights) to go, as well as the days, hours, minutes and seconds.

When is Easter this year?

Easter moves every year. The Easter Sunday countdown works out the date automatically and shows it under the title.

Does it work for other countries?

Yes. Choose a flag at the top and the list changes to that country’s holidays – for example Thanksgiving in the USA (fourth Thursday in November) or Thanksgiving in Canada (second Monday in October).

Can I count down to my own date?

Yes – press “Your own date”, give it a name, date and optional time, and pick an icon.
